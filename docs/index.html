Setup in a minute

Connect a service in three steps.

  1. Install with one Terminal command, or download the .zip.
  2. Allow Calendar access to show the Google, iCloud and Outlook accounts already on your Mac.
  3. Connect a Service, pick Canvas, Todoist or any other, and paste the link it tells you where to find.

FAQ

Questions

Is UMST really free?

Yes. UMST is free and open source under the MIT license. There's no paid tier, subscription or account.

How do I get my Canvas deadlines on my Mac desktop?

In Canvas, open Calendar and click Calendar Feed, then copy the link. In UMST choose Connect a Service → Canvas and paste it. Your assignments and due dates show up next to your other calendars.

Does it work with Google Calendar and Gmail?

Yes. Add your Google account in System Settings → Internet Accounts with Calendars turned on, and UMST shows it, including invites from Gmail. You can also paste Google's secret iCal address.

Which Macs are supported?

Any Mac running macOS 14 Sonoma or later, with Apple silicon or Intel.

Why does macOS say UMST is from an unidentified developer?

UMST isn't notarized by Apple yet. The install command handles this for you. If you download the .zip instead, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway the first time.

How do I uninstall it?

Run the uninstall command in the README. It removes the app, its settings, its data, its Keychain items and its login item.

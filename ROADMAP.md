# Roadmap

UMST's direction: **one calm place on your Mac desktop for everything you have to do and everywhere you have to be**: classes, deadlines, meetings, tasks. It stays free, open source, private and focused on the calendar.

Track progress in [milestones](https://github.com/nicoleyangcoding/UMST-UltimateMacSchedulingTool/milestones) and [issues](https://github.com/nicoleyangcoding/UMST-UltimateMacSchedulingTool/issues?q=is%3Aopen+label%3Aroadmap). Ideas and 👍 reactions on issues help decide what comes next.
